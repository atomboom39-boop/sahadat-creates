Process

Four steps, done in order.

  1. 01

    Tell me about the brand.

    A short call or a few messages. What you sell, who you sell to, what keeps you up at night.

  2. 02

    I make a sample post.

    Free, two days, no strings. The same work you would pay for, so you can judge the real thing.

  3. 03

    You approve, I repeat.

    If it lands, we agree a plan and a rhythm. Weekly, monthly — your call, not mine.

  4. 04

    Content ships on schedule.

    The same standard as the sample, delivered every single week on time.
